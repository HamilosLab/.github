# Hamilos Lab

Research lab led by **Allison Hamilos** at the [Whitehead Institute](https://wi.mit.edu/), MIT.

🔗 [hamilos.wi.mit.edu](https://hamilos.wi.mit.edu/)

## Research Focus

The Hamilos Lab studies how neural circuits enable spontaneous decision-making under uncertainty — how the brain chooses when it can't know the right answer. We aim to dissect the neural mechanisms of probabilistic reasoning and stochastic behavior, and how these processes go awry in neurological and psychiatric disease.

**Techniques:** behavioral training in mice, neurosurgery, optogenetics and neural recording, computational modeling.
